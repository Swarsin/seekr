# Espresso dial-in log

Start with a dose of 18 grams and aim for a 36 gram yield in roughly 25 to 30
seconds. If the shot runs too fast and tastes sour, grind finer. If it runs too
slow and tastes bitter or dry, grind coarser. Change one variable at a time and
write down the grinder setting, dose, yield and time.

Fresh beans release carbon dioxide and can make the first days of a new bag
unpredictable. Let rest days pass, then adjust the grind again. Keep the basket
clean and purge a little coffee after each grinder adjustment so the next shot
reflects the new setting.
