# Sourdough starter notes

A healthy starter is a stable community of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria.
Keep it at roughly 100% hydration: equal weights of flour and water. Feed it
once a day at room temperature, or weekly in the refrigerator, and let it warm
up before baking. The starter is ready when it doubles within four to eight
hours and smells pleasantly sour, not like acetone.

If a dark liquid forms on top, that is alcohol called hooch. Pour it off or stir
it in, then feed more regularly. Never keep a starter in a sealed jar because
carbon dioxide needs to escape. Use a clean jar and a loose lid or cloth.
