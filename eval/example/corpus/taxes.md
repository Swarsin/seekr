# Self-assessment paperwork

The UK self-assessment online filing deadline is 31 January following the end
of the tax year. Late filing immediately triggers a fixed penalty, and interest
accrues on unpaid tax. Register for self-assessment early if this is your first
return; the registration deadline is earlier than the filing deadline.

Keep records of income, expenses, pension contributions and charitable gifts.
Use the government gateway to submit the return and choose a payment method.
If you cannot pay in full, contact HMRC before the deadline to discuss a time to
pay arrangement rather than ignoring letters.
