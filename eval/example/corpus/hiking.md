# Cold-weather day hiking

For a cold mountain day hike, carry layers rather than one heavy jacket: a
moisture-wicking base layer, an insulating fleece or down mid layer, and a
windproof waterproof shell. Pack gloves, a warm hat, spare socks and a headlamp.
Weather can change quickly above the tree line, so check the forecast and turn
around before visibility disappears.

Carry more water than feels necessary in summer, plus high-energy snacks and a
small emergency shelter. Tell someone your route and expected return time. A map,
compass or charged offline phone map is essential where mobile coverage is
unreliable. Boots should be broken in and have enough room for thick socks.
